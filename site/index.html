01 Multimodal AI · Deep learning · 2024

SENTIMENT-X multimodal sentiment analysis

Emotion isn’t only in words. SENTIMENT-X recognizes it across video, audio and text at once — fusing facial expression, tone of voice and language into a single reading.

  • 92%accuracy
  • 60%latency reduction
  • 3modalities — video, audio, text
  • PyTorch
  • CNN
  • Transformers
  • DistilBERT
  • OpenCV
  • Tkinter

How it works

  • A CNN reads facial expressions from video frames.
  • A CNN–Transformer hybrid models speech for tone of voice.
  • A fine-tuned DistilBERT classifies the text.
  • The three signals are fused for real-time, comprehensive emotion detection in a desktop app.

02 Generative AI · Computer vision

Synthetic Face Generation with DCGAN

Generated faces are getting impossible to tell apart from real ones. This DCGAN creates them — and stamps each with an invisible watermark so AI-made content stays traceable.

  • 200K+CelebA training images
  • 18.4FID score
  • 95.8%watermark detection on a 10K-image test set
  • PyTorch
  • DCGAN
  • GANs
  • CelebA
  • Watermarking

What I built

  • A deep convolutional GAN trained on 200K+ CelebA face images.
  • Invisible watermarking of generated images for AI-content traceability.
  • Evaluated with FID (18.4) and watermark-detection accuracy (95.8% on 10K images).
